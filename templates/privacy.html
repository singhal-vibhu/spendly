Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information that you provide directly to us. This includes your account credentials (email and password) and expense data that you choose to log, such as transaction amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect or store any information about your bank accounts, credit card numbers, or other sensitive financial information.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely for the purpose of providing and improving our service. Your expense data is used to generate personal spending summaries, category breakdowns, and time-based filters for your own viewing. We do not use your data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers with industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or other operational purposes. These third parties may have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to maintain confidentiality. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You can export or delete your expense data through your account settings. If you choose to delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us. We are committed to addressing your concerns and ensuring your privacy is protected.
